NEW ADVERSARIES 0.5.0
A mod of Seven Kingdoms: Ancient Adversaries
============================================

New Adversaries is a mod of the strategy game Seven Kingdoms: Ancient
Adversaries, based on the open source version 7kaa 2.15.7 by 7kfans
(https://7kfans.com/). It is not an official product: Seven Kingdoms is a
trademark of Enlight Software.

This installer includes the full game; the original game doesn't need to be
installed.


WHICH EXECUTABLE TO USE
-----------------------

New Adversaries (7k-newAdversaries.exe)
    The full mod. It has everything in Classic plus rule changes:
    - Play as a Fryhtan kingdom ("Fryhtans" tab in New Game): train Fryhtans
      in your lairs, enslave villages and collect their tribute, plunder them
      with Raid and send your Ordos to subjugate them, kidnap villagers or work
      them harder. Earn Life Points by killing and research the technologies of
      your lairs.
    - Invoke Fryhtans from the Seat of Power; the AI uses them too.
    - Greater Beings with new powers, extended espionage (propaganda,
      accidents, intelligence reports), raw material trade at the harbor,
      Train Captain in towns, new technologies and race balance.
    This is the one we recommend.

New Adversaries Classic (7k-newAdversaries-Classic.exe)
    The original game rules, with interface and quality of life
    improvements:
    - In-game scenario editor.
    - Recruit and train from the fort, mines, factories and towers of
      science, with an auto recruit button.
    - List of the selected units, box selection that prefers military units,
      new keyboard shortcuts and fixes.
    For players who want the classic game, but more comfortable.

The saved games of one can't be opened in the other.


SAVED GAMES AND SETTINGS
------------------------

Each executable keeps its saved games and settings in its own folder:

    New Adversaries:          %APPDATA%\7kfans.com\7k-newAdversaries\
    New Adversaries Classic:  %APPDATA%\7kfans.com\7k-newAdversaries-Classic\

(Type %APPDATA%\7kfans.com in the address bar of the File Explorer to open
it.) They don't clash with the original game if it's installed too.
Uninstalling the mod doesn't delete these folders.


DOCUMENTATION
-------------

In the game folder:

    Changelog\   Changes of each version (7kmod = Classic,
                 gameplay = New Adversaries, fryhtan = the Fryhtan kingdom).
    Docs\        The players' guide (GUIDE: everything the mod changes, by
                 topic), keyboard shortcuts (KEYBINDINGS), Greater Beings,
                 espionage (SPYING), siege weapons (SIEGE_WEAPONS) and the
                 advanced config.txt options (ADVANCED_CONFIG).
    7kaa-manual.pdf   The manual of the original game.

The files ending in .en.txt are the English version; the others are in
Spanish.
